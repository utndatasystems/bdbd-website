About BDBD

The Bavarian Database Day (BDBD) is an annual one-day workshop bringing together researchers and professionals from the database community to present current work, discuss future directions, and connect with one another.

The 2026 edition focuses on talks from the academic database community, with a particular emphasis on junior researchers.

Organizers

Bavarian Database Day 2026 is organized by Marcus Paradies and Andreas Kipf.
